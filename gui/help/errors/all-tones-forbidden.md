# Every tone is forbidden

Every relative pitch from 1 to the [octave division](fields/octave-division) is listed in [forbidden tones](fields/harmony-forbidden-tones).

## Why this matters

XCL-FRQ (EMR-3 8.2, entry 23) excludes specific tones from the INTERVAL principle's output - but at least one tone has to remain available for it to produce anything at all. Forbidding every possible tone leaves nothing the principle could ever select, not even for its very first (randomly chosen) tone.

## How to fix it

- Remove at least one entry from the forbidden-tones list.
- If you intended to forbid *most* tones, double check the list doesn't accidentally also include every tone the matrix would otherwise reach - a few forbidden tones is normal and expected, all of them is not.
