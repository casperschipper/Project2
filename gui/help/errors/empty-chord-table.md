# Empty chord table

The [CHORD principle](fields/harmony-principle) is active, but the [chord table](fields/harmony-chord) has no chords in it at all.

## Why this matters

CHORD draws a whole chord from the table at every entry point - with no chords to draw from, there is nothing for HARMONY to produce, and no way to know how many notes any entry point should have.

## How to fix it

Add at least one chord. A chord made entirely of `p` (percussion) is allowed, if that's genuinely what you want.
