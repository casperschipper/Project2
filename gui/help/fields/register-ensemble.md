# Register ensemble

> How a group of registers is chosen from the table for each layer.

Same mechanism as every other parameter's ensemble principle - see [selection principles](concepts/selection-principles) for the general idea, and the [dynamics](fields/dynamics-table) or [duration](fields/duration-table) tables for worked examples of the same choice. `Combination` ties the chosen group to whichever instrument group is active, which is usually what you want here: it is how a percussion instrument's group and the percussion register entry end up paired together reliably rather than by chance.
