# Forbidden tones

> XCL-FRQ, EMR-3 8.2 entry 23: relative pitches the interval principle may never produce.

Any tone listed here is excluded from the INTERVAL principle's chain entirely - not just as a successor to some other tone, but even as the very first (randomly chosen) tone the chain starts from. This is the one admission condition ([see matrix](fields/harmony-matrix)) that is never relaxed, however restrictive the matrix turns out to be.

Only forbid a tone if the matrix leaves at least one alternative for it somewhere - forbidding every tone up to the [octave division](fields/octave-division) leaves nothing the principle could ever produce (see [all tones forbidden](errors/all-tones-forbidden)).
