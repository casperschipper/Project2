# Register order

> How successive registers are drawn from within the active group.

Same mechanism as every other parameter's order principle - see [selection principles](concepts/selection-principles). Under `series`, a group containing both the percussion entry and a pitched range alternates unpitched and pitched material evenly; under `ratio`, you can weight how often percussion is chosen relative to pitched ranges; a [tendency](concepts/tendency) can gradually move a layer from percussion-heavy to fully pitched (or the reverse) over the course of a variant.
