# Rest order

> How successive rest lengths are drawn from the active group, one per rest actually placed.

Once the [ensemble](fields/rest-ensemble) has fixed which lengths are available, this principle decides the succession of lengths across however many rests [rest mode](fields/rest-mode)'s search ends up placing in this layer. All six [selection principles](concepts/selection-principles) are available, though most compose better here than [tendency](concepts/tendency) does: a tendency window's schedule is sized against a event count decided in advance, but the true number of rests only becomes known once the search has actually run, so a tendency-ordered rest length is a reasonable approximation rather than an exact fit.

**Series** or **alea** are the natural defaults — a rotating or randomised vocabulary of pause lengths.

**Ratio** favours one rest length over the others, giving the piece a characteristic "kind" of silence with occasional longer or shorter interruptions.

**Group** and **sequence** work as they do everywhere: fixed runs of one length, or a written-out succession.
