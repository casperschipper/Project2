# Invert matrix

> BIT, EMR-3 8.2 entry 24: flip every cell of the resulting matrix.

A simple post-processing toggle applied to whichever [matrix](fields/harmony-matrix) resulted - hand-toggled or derived from a chord. Turning it on makes every previously forbidden transition allowed, and every previously allowed transition forbidden. [Forbidden tones](fields/harmony-forbidden-tones) are unaffected - inversion only ever touches the matrix of intervals, never the separate list of forbidden tones.
