9.3 THE PITCH-TIME GRID |
The pitch-time grid, like the PERFORMANCE parameter, is a later
addition to PR-2. The three variables for the pitch-time grid were
originally constants. Fig. 9-7 is a survey of the entries.
The pitch-time grid provides the composer with the opportunity of
(a) — determining the number of tones per octave; this number is the |
. same for all octaves,
(b) determining the maximum number of metrical subdivisions; this
information is only taken into consideration for the printout
of the parts, and is of no account for the score (in seconds),
(c) fixing the smallest time-value which is applicable to the sub-
division of the metre (triplets, quintuplets, etc.). All sub-
divisions below this limit value are then ignored.
The tones within the octave are numbered; the composer is free
to interpret the numbers of the tones. Since the octave numbers are
integral multiples of 102 for the representation of "absolute" pitches,
a maximum of 99 tones per octave is available.
For the printout of the parts, all time-values are converted
into metric expressions. For this purpose the duration of the metre is
first calculated according to the metronome tempo: dm = 60/tempo. The
absolute durations of the metric subdivisions are accordingly dm/1,
dm/2, dm/3, ..., dm/fr.
