As soon as a chord has been used, it is transposed and inserted

. in the table again. The starting tones of the chords given by the com-
poser are stored separately and used as reference tones for transposition.
Transposition is carried out by means of a transposition interval made
available by ALEA, SERIES or a tone-row given by the composer. The chord-
tones in the form prescribed by the composer are raised by the transpos-
ition interval modulo tr (according to the reference tones). Zeros (= no
pitch) are ignored. ALEA and SERIES provide transposition intervals bet-
ween 1 and tr (transposition by tr is the same as transposition by 0,
thus resulting in the original pitch); the composer's transposition row
may also only contain relative pitches between i and tr inclusive.
