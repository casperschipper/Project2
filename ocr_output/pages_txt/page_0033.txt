The first permutation occurs when the elements of the list are |

first selection. Since the composer makes the table himself, he makes :

The second permutation occurs when the table-groups are formed
into an ensemble. For this, selection programmes are employed; they |
determine on the one hand the number of groups and on the other hand |
the selection of the groups. Since the ensemble is formed again for
each variant, a permutation can occur for each variant. )
