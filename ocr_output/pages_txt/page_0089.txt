Survey of DYNAMICS parameter |
The mode refers to the ratio of the dynamics within a chord.
If we have MOD-DYN = 0, only one intensity is selected for each entry-
point (and thus for all tones starting at the same time), whilst
MOD-DYN = 1 means that a new intensity is inserted for each single
tone (regardless, then, of the distribution of the tones in chords).
