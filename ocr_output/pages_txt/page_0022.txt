2.3 ENSEMBLE

When a variant is worked out, normally one group per parameter
is selected and registered in ENSEMBLE. The ensemble then contains
either the entire list or at least a selection represented by the
selected group. Under certain circumstances (see section 6) several

| groups can be put together into an ensemble. The groups are then registered
in the ensemble in the same order in which they are named according to
the given permutation principle (see example 2-1).

The order of the elements (represented by their list indices)
in the ensemble is the final arrangement for the variant. The use of
this arrangement for purposes of data selection for the score depends
on the permutation principle chosen for this.
